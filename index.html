AI Engineer with a Builder's Mindset

My journey into AI didn't start with a fancy degree — it started with curiosity and late nights debugging code. After completing my BCA from Deogiri College, I knew I wanted to build systems that could think, learn, and solve real problems.

What began with regression algorithms quickly evolved into working with Large Language Models, building RAG systems, and creating autonomous AI agents. The shift from traditional ML to Generative AI felt natural — I was fascinated by how language models could be chained to perform complex tasks.

Today I build production-ready AI applications using LangChain, LangGraph, FastAPI, and modern LLM frameworks. My strength lies in bridging the gap between AI research and deployable software.
